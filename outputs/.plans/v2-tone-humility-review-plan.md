# Review plan — v2-tone-humility

## Artifact
- `v2/optimal-trading-filters-v2.tex` (local LaTeX manuscript, 18 pp. compiled)
- Review focus (user-directed): prose tone. The text reads as LLM-generated —
  over-confident pronouncements ("becomes the operative constraint"), aphoristic
  X-without-Y / punchline constructions, every paragraph landing a verdict.
  Goal: identify passages and supply humbler, human-register rewrites.

## Review criteria (scoped to this run)
- Writing quality is the primary axis. Novelty/rigor/baselines/reproducibility were
  covered in the prior review (`outputs/optimal-trading-filters-v2-review.md`) and are
  NOT re-litigated here; claims validity enters only to separate earned confidence
  (proved/verified statements) from unearned interpretive pronouncements.
- Tic inventory to hunt:
  1. Verdict sentences: "becomes the operative constraint", "the whole difficulty",
     "is precisely because", "exactly when", "is the single fact/number".
  2. Epigram/punchline cadence: sentence-final mic-drops ("...and is then forgotten",
     "...one that never fully forgets", "the singularity any impact term regularizes").
  3. Over-parallel aphorisms: "Short-memory impact produces a short-memory policy;
     long-memory impact produces one that never fully forgets."
  4. Absolute quantifiers where a hedge is human: every/never/always/exactly/precisely.
  5. Dramatic appositions and triads: "anticausally whiten, project, causally color".
  6. Zero hedging, zero first-person: no "we", no "appears", no "in our setting".

## Verification checks
- Grep-based inventory of each tic with line numbers (done; in evidence notes).
- Distinguish: statement backed by a theorem/numerical check (confidence earned; may
  keep declarative form) vs interpretive gloss stated as law (should soften).
- Confirm no factual claims are changed by proposed rewrites.

## Method
Lead-owned. Author-adversarial pass (much of this prose originated in this session, so
the review deliberately treats it as hostile material). Evidence notes first, then a
single review with per-passage rewrites.
