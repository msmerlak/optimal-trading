### Known issue found during drafting, still in the text
- §2.3 (line ~213): "The corresponding position filter χ̂/(−iω) lies in L² for every kernel."
  Numerical check this session (session transcript; script run): for pure power-law η=λ=0 with
  OU signal, |x̂/α̂|² ~ ω^{2ν−2} near 0 and the position VARIANCE integral diverges
  (λ=0 divergent; λ=0.1 → 0.63; λ=1.0 → 0.18 finite). The per-kernel L² claim needs the
  qualification λ>0 (or a signal-side decay condition) for the pure power-law case. The paper's
  own limitations paragraph (line ~438) acknowledges unbounded factors for pure power-law on a
  dense domain, but the §2.3 sentence as written is unqualified.

### Claims not independently verified (analytical, plausible, proof sketches in appendix)
- Lemma 1 proof (App A): triangular bookkeeping; relies on P₊Q₊ preservation; for unbounded
  power-law factors deferred to dense-domain hypothesis. Rigor level: sketch, adequate for a
  QF-style paper but a functional-analysis referee may want the nest-algebra details or a
  citation to Arveson's theorem with hypotheses matched.
- Prop 3 (boundary-layer decay): constants C(β), C not given; proof (App E) is an estimate
  sketch (truncation of Marchaud tail; weight deviation subdominance). Paper admits "not yet
  sharp constants" (§7). Fig 5 + check 7 support the qualitative statement.
- Duality remark (eq:dual analog): whole-line pairing E⟨·,·⟩ formally infinite for stationary
  signals; per-unit-time reading is implied but not stated in the remark itself (was flagged
  during drafting; remark kept short).
