## Review

### Correct

- **Eq. (9) factorization (line 93).** $\hat C_\pm(\xi)=c_\gamma^{1/2}(\pm i\xi)^{-\beta}$ with $\beta=(1-\gamma)/2$ multiplies to $c_\gamma|\xi|^{-2\beta}=c_\gamma|\xi|^{\gamma-1}=\hat G(\xi)$. Half-plane assignment ($\hat C_+$ analytic on upper half-plane, $\hat C_-$ on lower) is consistent with the SKM $\mathcal{F}[f](\xi)=\int f(t)e^{i\xi t}dt$ convention used implicitly (matches short paper App. B, line 204–205).
- **Eq. (10) time-domain factors (line 97).** $C_\pm=c_\gamma^{1/2}I_\pm^\beta$ is consistent with the Fourier symbols of the Riemann–Liouville integrals (SKM §5.1); their inverses $C_\pm^{-1}=c_\gamma^{-1/2}D_\pm^\beta$ agree with §2.6 Steps 1 & 3 (lines 127, 135) and with the boxed identity in the short paper (line 40).
- **Adjoint relation $C_+^\ast=C_-$ (Lemma 1 hypothesis line 103; Table 1 (iii) line 176).** Correct: $(I_+^\beta)^\ast=I_-^\beta$ on $L^2(\mathbb{R})$. Consistent with the Marchaud pairing $\langle D_+^\beta f,g\rangle=\langle f,D_-^\beta g\rangle$. No sign issue.
- **Constant $\kappa_{1-\gamma}=[2c\,\Gamma(1-\gamma)\sin(\pi\gamma/2)]^{-1}$ (line 119).** Matches the standard Fourier symbol $\mathcal{F}[|t|^{-\gamma}](\xi)=2\Gamma(1-\gamma)\sin(\pi\gamma/2)|\xi|^{\gamma-1}$ (Gel'fand–Shilov / SKM §7.1). $c_\gamma$ definition is identical to the short paper (line 40) and $\kappa_{1-\gamma}=c_\gamma^{-1}$ is used consistently in Eq. (6) (line 63) and Steps 1/3.
- **Theorem 1, Step (c) symbol identity (line 226).** $\hat G(\xi)(i\xi)^\beta = c_\gamma|\xi|^{\gamma-1+\beta}e^{i\beta\pi\,\text{sgn}(\xi)/2} = c_\gamma(-i\xi)^{-\beta}$ (uses $\gamma-1+\beta=-\beta$). Identical to short paper Eq. (B.2), line 208. Time-domain corollary $G\ast D_+^\beta = c_\gamma I_-^\beta$ is correct.
- **Theorem 1 closure (line 228).** With $\kappa_{1-\gamma}=c_\gamma^{-1}$, $\mathbb{E}_t[(Cu^{\rm cand})(t)]=\kappa_{1-\gamma}\mathbb{E}_t[(G\ast D_+^\beta\zeta)(t)]=c_\gamma^{-1}\cdot c_\gamma\,(I_-^\beta\hat\zeta_t)(t)=(I_-^\beta\hat\zeta_t)(t)$. Then Step (b) (line 223) + $I_-^\beta$-locality on $s\ge t$ + $I_-^\beta D_-^\beta=\mathrm{id}$ on $H^\beta(\mathbb{R})$ (SKM §5.3 Thm 5.3) close to $\bar\alpha(t,t)=\alpha_t$. Chain is correct and matches short paper Steps 1–3 (lines 157–170).
- **OU calculation Eq. (14) (line 143).** For $\bar\alpha(t,s)=e^{-\theta(s-t)}\alpha_t$ on $s\ge t$, direct Marchaud gives $(D_-^\beta\bar\alpha(t,\cdot))(t)=\alpha_t\cdot\frac{\beta}{\Gamma(1-\beta)}\int_0^\infty (e^{-\theta h}-1)h^{-1-\beta}dh=\alpha_t\cdot\frac{\beta}{\Gamma(1-\beta)}\cdot\frac{\Gamma(1-\beta)}{\beta}\theta^\beta=\theta^\beta\alpha_t$ (using $-\Gamma(-\beta)\theta^\beta=\Gamma(1-\beta)\theta^\beta/\beta$). Correct, agrees with short paper Remark 1 (line 61).
- **Kernel-symbol consistency.** §2.1 writes $G(t)=c|t|^{-\gamma}$ (symmetric convolution appropriate for the cost quadratic form $\iint G(|t-v|)u_tu_v$), while the abstract/§1 write the one-sided propagator $G(t)=ct^{-\gamma}$. These agree on $t>0$ and produce the same symmetric symbol $\hat G(\xi)=c_\gamma|\xi|^{\gamma-1}$. No error.

### Blockers

- **Proof of Lemma 1 in §5 is incoherent as written (line 218).** Three separate defects:
  1. "$C=C_-C_+$ is bounded and invertible with bounded inverse $C^{-1}=C_+^{-1}C_-^{-1}$." False on $L^2(\mathbb{R})$: $C$ is a Riesz-type convolution with symbol $c_\gamma|\xi|^{\gamma-1}$ (unbounded near $\xi=0$), and its inverse has symbol $c_\gamma^{-1}|\xi|^{1-\gamma}$ (unbounded near $\infty$). Both operators are only bounded between appropriate homogeneous Sobolev spaces $\dot H^{(1-\gamma)/2}\leftrightarrow \dot H^{-(1-\gamma)/2}$. The Lemma should be stated on this Sobolev pair (or on the dense subspace where compositions are defined), not on $L^2(\mathbb{R})$.
  2. Derivation "Applying $C_+^{-1}$ … yields $P_+C_-^{-1}(f)=C_+u-C_+P_+^\perp u=C_+u$" is a genuine slip. Applying $C_+^{-1}$ to $f=P_+CP_+u=P_+C_-C_+u$ gives $C_+^{-1}P_+C_-C_+u$, not $P_+C_-^{-1}f$. The RHS "$C_+u-C_+P_+^\perp u$" is confusing: since $u\in L^2_{\rm adap}$, $P_+^\perp u=0$ tautologically, so the term "$C_+P_+^\perp u$" carries no content. Then "Applying $C_+^{-1}$ once more" produces $u=C_+^{-1}\cdot(C_+u)$, but at that point one has not established the equality $P_+C_-^{-1}f=C_+u$ that the next step needs.
  3. The clean argument (which the paper should give) uses the two "triangularity" identities that follow from causality/anticausality: $P_+^\perp C_+ P_+ = 0$ (because $C_+$ preserves $L^2_{\rm adap}$) and, by adjoint, $P_+ C_- P_+^\perp = 0$. From the first, for adapted $u$: $C_+ u \in L^2_{\rm adap}$, so $P_+ C_+ u = C_+ u$ and $C_+^{-1}$ commutes with $P_+$ on adapted inputs. From the second, $P_+ C_-^{-1} = P_+ C_-^{-1} P_+$ (by taking the same identity for the anticausal inverse $C_-^{-1}$). Then $C_+^{-1}P_+C_-^{-1}\cdot P_+CP_+ u = C_+^{-1}P_+C_-^{-1}P_+C_-C_+u = C_+^{-1}\cdot P_+C_-^{-1}P_+C_-\cdot(C_+u) = C_+^{-1}\cdot(C_+u)=u$, using $P_+C_-^{-1}P_+C_- = P_+C_-^{-1}\cdot C_-P_+$ on the adapted vector $C_+u$ via $P_+C_-P_+=P_+C_-$ (adjoint of $P_+^\perp C_+P_+=0$) and $C_-^{-1}C_-=\mathrm{id}$. This is the standard nest-algebra argument the paper cites in (10) ch. 8 and (17); it should be written out even at compressed length.

  Because both this and the "bounded inverse" claim occur in one dense paragraph, a referee will treat the entire Lemma 1 proof as unproven. This is the most exposed math in the paper (it is what makes Eq. (5)/Table 1 rigorous, and it is the piece with no direct analog in the short paper's App. A — the short paper proves Theorem 1 by direct FOC verification, bypassing Lemma 1 entirely).

### Fixes worth doing now

- **Rewrite the Lemma 1 proof** using the two triangularity identities above. A three-to-five-sentence version fits the PNAS format and dispatches all three defects at once. If space is tight, state the Sobolev domain in §2.3 ("$C:\dot H^{-\beta}\to\dot H^\beta$ is a Hilbert-space isomorphism") and refer the reader to the companion long paper's Lemma for the nest-algebra proof.
- **§2.1 kernel notation (line 79).** Since $G(t)=c|t|^{-\gamma}$ (symmetric) is used but the intro repeats $G(t)=ct^{-\gamma}$ (one-sided propagator), add one clause: "we identify $G$ with its even extension for the symmetric convolution in (2)." Prevents a referee objection.
- **Step (b) reference (line 223).** The claim "for $s>t$, $\hat\zeta_t(s)=(D_-^\beta\bar\alpha(t,\cdot))(s)$" invokes tower + conditional Fubini at conditioning time $t$. The short paper's Lemma 1 (line 48 there) is the correct citation for the commutation of $\mathbb{E}_t$ with the Marchaud integral; the PNAS paper cites only Klenke Thm 14.16 (generic conditional Fubini), which is defensible but weaker. Add a pointer to the companion paper's Lemma 1 for the finite Marchaud modulus of continuity.
- **Injectivity statement (line 218).** "Strict positivity of $\hat G$ on $\xi\ne 0$" is fine as the criterion for injectivity of $C$; state it as strict convexity of the cost quadratic form on $L^2_{\rm adap}$ (Uniqueness paragraph line 232 already does this properly — quote/reuse that phrasing in Lemma 1).

### Notes / optional

- **Eq. (13) commutation (line 133).** The reference "(19, Prop. 2.6.13)" (Hytönen–van Neerven–Veraar–Weis) covers commutation of conditional expectation with deterministic bounded operators. Since $D_-^\beta$ is unbounded on $L^2$ (bounded only $H^\beta\to L^2$), the strictly correct hypothesis is that $\bar\alpha(s,\cdot)$ is $H^\beta$-valued a.s. The abstract §2.1 integrability hypothesis $\int(1+|\xi|^{2(1-\gamma)+\epsilon})S_\alpha\,d\xi<\infty$ implies this, but stating it explicitly at (13) is cheap insurance against a referee objection.
- **Step (a) (line 222).** Applying conditional Fubini directly to the Marchaud representation of $D_+^\beta$ requires the $L^1$-integrability of the increment quotient shown in the short paper (line 159). A one-line remark that "under the stationary spectral hypothesis of §2.1, the modulus estimate $\mathbb{E}|\zeta_{v-h}-\zeta_v|\le Ch^{\beta+\epsilon/2}$ ensures Fubini applies" would parallel the short paper and pre-empt the standard Marchaud-domain question.
- **"Bounded and invertible" (line 218).** Replace by "densely defined and formally invertible on Schwartz class, with the identity extending to $\dot H^{-\beta}\to\dot H^\beta$ by Plancherel." Same short paper convention.
- **Table 1 row "Right factor / Left factor" (lines 164–165).** With $u^\star=C_+^{-1}P_+C_-^{-1}\alpha$ read right-to-left, $C_-^{-1}$ is applied first ("right factor"), $C_+^{-1}$ last ("left factor"). The table labels are consistent with this reading. No error, but the parenthetical "$L^{-1}$ (cross-sectional whitening)" versus "$C_-^{-1}$ (anticausal whitening)" invites confusion because a reader might expect $C_+^{-1}$ (causal, the one the trader actually deploys against past data) to be the whitening step; the temporal whitening is genuinely $\zeta = C_-^{-1}\bar\alpha$ per §3 (iv). Consider a footnote noting that only the projected $\zeta_s = (P_+ C_-^{-1}\alpha)(s)$ is realizable — the whitening happens on the forecast curve, not on the realized path.
- **§2.3 kernel of $C_+$ (line 99).** "$C_+$ causal (kernel supported on $\{s\le t\}$)" — the RL integral $I_+^\beta$ has $(I_+^\beta f)(t)=\Gamma(\beta)^{-1}\int_{-\infty}^t (t-s)^{\beta-1}f(s)\,ds$, so the kernel is $(t-s)^{\beta-1}\mathbf 1_{s\le t}$, matching the statement. Correct.
- **Injectivity condition wording (line 218).** "on $\xi\ne 0$" is technically correct but a referee may flag that the constant mode is not in $L^2_{\rm adap}$ anyway, so the $\xi=0$ exclusion is harmless — worth one clause.

## Summary

The mathematical content of Eq. (9)–(10), Theorem 1 (including the pivotal symbol identity, the $I_-^\beta D_-^\beta$ inversion on $H^\beta$, and Step (b) tower argument), the OU calculation (14), and the constant $\kappa_{1-\gamma}$ are all correct and internally consistent with the short paper's ground-truth statements and proofs (short paper lines 40, 54–57, 61, 157–170, 204–211). Sign conventions and half-plane assignments are consistent.

The single blocker is the **proof of Lemma 1 in §5 (line 218)**: it contains a false boundedness claim and a muddled composition step, and needs to be rewritten with the standard triangularity identities $P_+^\perp C_+P_+=0$ and $P_+C_-P_+^\perp=0$ before the paper goes out for review. All other items are minor tightening.
